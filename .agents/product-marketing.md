# PhotoDays product marketing

## Positioning

PhotoDays is a private visual progress journal for iPhone and iPad. It helps people turn scattered progress photos into aligned comparisons and a replayable visual story.

Primary promise:

> See the change you’re too close to notice.

## Audience

- People tracking slow visual changes in fitness, recovery, hair, skincare or personal style.
- Parents and families documenting growth.
- Pet owners, plant keepers and people improving a space.
- Makers and artists recording a long creative project.
- Anyone who has progress photos buried in their camera roll and cannot easily see the story.

## Problems to name

- Slow change is hard to notice day to day.
- Photos are scattered and difficult to compare fairly.
- Inconsistent framing makes honest comparison harder.
- A rigid daily habit can create pressure instead of motivation.
- Months of photos do not automatically become a meaningful story.

## Outcomes to promise

- Visual proof that effort is producing change.
- One organized timeline for each journey.
- A flexible rhythm supported by reminders and streaks.
- Aligned before-and-after comparisons.
- A progress film made from the user’s own moments.
- Clear album-level charts that reveal direction and patterns over time.
- App-wide achievements that make consistency feel visible and rewarding.
- Private, local-first storage with optional iCloud and Face ID protection.

## Approved product claims

- PhotoDays can organize photos into dedicated visual journals.
- The camera can use a previous photo and alignment guidance for consistent framing.
- Users can compare two dates.
- PhotoDays can create progress videos and GIFs.
- Each album can visualize recorded progress in charts.
- Streaks, achievements and the achievement profile span the app’s journeys.
- Photos and progress data are stored on the device.
- iCloud sync is optional.
- Face ID can protect the app or individual albums.
- The website has no analytics, cookies, account creation or email capture.

## Forbidden or unverified claims

- Ratings, testimonials, user counts or awards.
- Medical, clinical, diagnostic or guaranteed outcome claims.
- AI analysis or automated health interpretation.
- Android or web-app availability.
- Final pricing or specific Premium terms before release.
- “Military-grade”, “end-to-end encrypted” or other unsupported security language.
- Claims that every person must photograph daily.

## Voice

Clear, warm, observant and calm. Lead with the human outcome, then use the feature as evidence. Prefer concrete words such as “compare”, “see”, “return” and “replay”. Avoid hype, productivity guilt and technical jargon.

## CTA

Before App Store release: `Coming soon on the App Store`.

Secondary landing-page action: `Watch the 25-second demo`.

Do not add a fake link, waitlist, email collection or purchase CTA until a real destination exists.
